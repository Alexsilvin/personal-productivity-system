# Vision

## Problem

People create plans in calm moments, then lose trust in them when meetings, interruptions, energy changes, or urgent work appear. Existing tools often record tasks but do not help users renegotiate the day.

## Vision

Create a calm planning partner that turns intentions into realistic time, continuously adapts plans to current conditions, and explains every meaningful change.

## Target users

- Knowledge workers balancing focused work and reactive responsibilities.
- People managing multiple projects with different priorities.
- Users who need structure without a rigid, over-optimized schedule.

## Success signals

- Users complete more of their highest-value work.
- Rescheduling requires less manual effort.
- Users understand why a plan changed.
- Notifications support action without fragmenting attention.
