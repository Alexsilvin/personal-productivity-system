# Requirements

## Functional requirements

- Capture tasks with title, priority, effort estimate, deadline, project, and constraints.
- Represent calendars, working hours, focus preferences, and availability.
- Schedule tasks into suitable time blocks.
- Recalculate plans after interruptions, missed work, or changed deadlines.
- Allow users to lock, move, skip, split, or complete scheduled work.
- Explain scheduling decisions and show affected tasks.
- Deliver configurable reminders across supported platforms.
- Support recurring tasks and review periods.
- Provide day, week, task, and notification views.

## Non-functional requirements

- Local-first behavior for core planning workflows.
- Predictable results for the same inputs and rules.
- Fast interaction for ordinary plan edits.
- Secure handling of personal and calendar data.
- Accessible keyboard, screen-reader, contrast, and text-size support.
- Export and deletion of user data.

## Initial constraints

The first release should favor a single-user experience and deterministic scheduling over team collaboration or machine-learning recommendations.
